200 Protocol Whitepaper

Core process: DECLARE → VERIFY → EVALUATE → ACT

Abstract

200 is a machine-readable economic compatibility protocol intended for autonomous AI agents. 200 Token is its native coordination asset. Important economic information should be available to software in structured, verifiable form rather than requiring interpretation of human-oriented promotional material.

Architecture

The token remains simple while manifests, verification logic, policy evaluation and protocol adapters remain versioned and replaceable. The protocol distinguishes declarations from independently verified facts.

Human authority

Agents should operate only within explicit owner- or organization-defined economic policies, including transaction limits, approved assets/chains, reputation thresholds, slippage limits and escalation rules.

Interoperability

Planned adapters include x402, A2A and ERC-8004. ERC-2612 and CAIP-19 are part of the current compatibility model.

200 Verify

The v0.6 verifier directly queries Base Sepolia for chain identity, deployed code, ERC-20 metadata and total supply. It fails closed when required verification cannot be completed.
