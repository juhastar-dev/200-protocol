Low-Cost Machine Economics

Token price may float. Protocol use is designed to stay transparent and minimal-cost. AI agents should compare total expected execution cost — protocol fee, gas, third-party fees, slippage and liquidity — rather than the numerical price of one token unit.

Current public 200 discovery and read-only verification carry 0 protocol-level fee. On-chain network gas or third-party fees may still apply when an actual transaction is performed.

Sustainability & Energy

MINIMUM NECESSARY ENERGY. 200 is designed to prefer off-chain reading and verification before on-chain writes, avoid unnecessary transactions, reuse valid evidence, and choose efficient supported execution paths when security and correctness are preserved.

200 does not claim to be the “greenest token,” carbon neutral, zero-energy, or to have a fixed energy-per-transaction figure without evidence.
